Put your own reminder sounds in this folder (mp3, ogg, wav, m4a, aac or opus).
Then list them in sounds.json, for example:

[
  {"name": "Morning bell", "file": "morning-bell.mp3"},
  {"name": "Gym horn", "file": "gym-horn.mp3"}
]

They appear in Settings > General > Habit & work-out alerts. Re-deploy the www folder after adding files.
