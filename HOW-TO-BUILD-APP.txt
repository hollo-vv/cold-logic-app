COLD LOGIC - get an Android app (APK) with no setup on your computer

1. On github.com create a NEW repository (for example: cold-logic-app). Keep it separate from your website repo.
2. Upload everything in this folder to it, INCLUDING the hidden-looking ".github" folder.
   If the ".github" folder does not upload: in the repo click Add file > Create new file, type
   .github/workflows/build-apk.yml as the name, and paste in the contents of that file.
3. Open the repo's "Actions" tab. If asked, click "I understand my workflows, go ahead and enable them".
4. Pick "Build Android APK" on the left and click "Run workflow". Wait about 5 to 8 minutes.
5. Open the finished run, scroll to "Artifacts", download "ColdLogic-APK". Unzip it to get app-debug.apk.
6. Send app-debug.apk to your phone and open it. Allow "Install unknown apps" when asked.
7. Open the app and allow notifications. In Settings > Apps > COLD LOGIC set battery to "Unrestricted",
   and allow "Alarms & reminders" so reminders arrive on time while the phone is locked.

Updating later: replace the files in the repo with the new www folder and run the workflow again.
